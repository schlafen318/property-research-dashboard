Miami / Fort Lauderdale: global access, coastal complexity

Miami / Fort Lauderdale offers an unusually complete warm-weather city-and-coast life: two international airports, major hospitals, established neighbourhoods, beaches and a deep resident economy. Yet the home matters more than the skyline. A Brickell tower, a Coral Gables house and a Galt Ocean condominium create different insurance, reserve, flood, mobility and resale decisions. This dossier identifies which pattern can support long-term life—and which costs and rules must be resolved before the view becomes persuasive.

By Global Home Atlas Research Team · Published 2026-08-24 · Reviewed 2026-08-24

The verdict

Miami / Fort Lauderdale is a strong retirement candidate for a buyer who wants international air access, year-round urban services and warm coastal living, but it is not a low-friction purchase. Property ownership does not create immigration status, Medicare eligibility or a right to remain in the United States. An ordinary home purchase is also not an EB-5 investment: that programme requires investment in a qualifying commercial enterprise and a plan to create or preserve ten permanent full-time jobs. Establish the residence route, health coverage, tax position and ownership structure before selecting a home.

Many foreign individuals can acquire Florida residential property, but the answer is no longer captured by saying that Florida is simply open. Part III of Chapter 692 restricts defined foreign principals and includes specific rules affecting persons and entities connected with foreign countries of concern, with particularly detailed provisions involving the People's Republic of China. Nationality, domicile, entity control, parcel location, proximity rules and statutory exceptions can change the outcome. Florida counsel should clear the exact buyer, entity and parcel before a non-refundable commitment.

The destination works best when the buyer chooses a daily-life system rather than a prestige label. Brickell offers rail, restaurants and an urban apartment routine, but association costs and tower governance are central. Coral Gables and Coconut Grove provide greener, lower-rise living with more private outdoor space, but driving, roof and envelope maintenance grow in importance. Miami Beach adds immediate coast and culture alongside stricter vacation-rental geography, salt exposure and flood diligence. Fort Lauderdale and Galt Ocean can provide a calmer coastal base with FLL and Broward Health nearby, but older-building records, reserves, insurance and assessments require exacting review.

Proceed only after four written answers exist: the buyer may lawfully acquire and remain; the building or house is insurable at a known price; the association, inspection and reserve record supports the budget; and the intended rental or personal use is permitted by the municipality and governing documents. When those answers are sound, Miami / Fort Lauderdale can be a credible year-round home. Without them, apparent liquidity and sunshine can conceal a costly operating mismatch.

Miami / Fort Lauderdale through five destination lenses

Five questions separate a durable South Florida home from a seductive but fragile purchase: whether ordinary life works, which airport and health network serve the address, what Florida and condominium rules require, whether income survives legal and operating costs, and who can buy the property on exit.

Build a daily life that works beyond the waterfront

The destination's advantage is not only beach weather. Miami-Dade and Broward contain neighbourhood hospitals, supermarkets, restaurants, arts, parks and established residential communities that remain active throughout the year. Brickell and Downtown Miami deliver the most urban version: high-rise living, Metromover and Metrorail access, dense dining and a short route to the civic core. That can reduce daily driving, but tower elevators, valet systems, loading, visitor parking and association rules become part of ordinary life. Test the building at rush hour, during heavy rain and when one elevator is unavailable.

Coral Gables and Coconut Grove trade some vertical convenience for shade, gardens, smaller buildings and houses. The strongest streets make groceries, cafés and parks feel close; other addresses remain car-dependent despite an attractive map. A detached home gives control over space but transfers roof, drainage, trees, impact protection, pool and exterior maintenance to the owner. Miami Beach offers an immediately recognisable coastal life, yet causeway traffic, visitor pressure, salt and wind exposure can turn a short distance into a demanding routine. A retirement choice should be judged on the least glamorous weekly errands, not a weekend itinerary.

Fort Lauderdale has its own centre rather than functioning as a Miami suburb. Las Olas and Downtown combine restaurants, culture and nearby healthcare; Galt Ocean and Lauderdale-by-the-Sea create a more residential coast with grocery and beach access in selected pockets. Broward Health Medical Center is a 24-hour full-service hospital and Level I trauma centre, while Jackson Memorial is a large tertiary teaching hospital serving the Miami side. These institutions support the regional case, but the exact route matters. Drive from the candidate home to the preferred hospital in peak traffic and after a storm-warning closure.

Healthcare financing must be solved separately. Medicare generally serves people aged 65 or older who meet its citizenship or lawful-residency requirements; a newly arrived foreign retiree should not assume immediate eligibility. Price suitable private health coverage, prescriptions and out-of-network exposure before moving. Then test heat, shade, walking surfaces, hurricane preparation, social life outside winter and the practicality of living for a month without a car. Miami / Fort Lauderdale scores well because it offers genuine city depth, not because coastal life is effortless.

Use two gateways without understating the last mile

Miami International Airport is the stronger global gateway, especially toward Latin America and the Caribbean. Its May 2026 network statement listed 176 nonstop destinations and 91 carriers, while 2025 passenger volume exceeded 55 million. Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International handled about 32.2 million passengers in 2025 and gives Broward residents a materially shorter alternative for many domestic and international journeys. Airport choice should follow the address and route network: a bargain fare is less useful when causeway or interstate congestion consumes the advantage.

Brickell and Coral Gables normally align more naturally with MIA; Galt Ocean and central Fort Lauderdale align with FLL. Miami Beach, Aventura and Lauderdale-by-the-Sea sit between access stories that vary by time of day. Measure the front-door journey with luggage, not the straight-line distance. Confirm parking, ride-hail pickup, late arrivals, accessibility assistance and a backup route during construction or severe weather. South Florida's regional scale is an asset only when the chosen home connects cleanly to the services the household actually uses.

Foreigner fit is high in language, professional services and international familiarity, but administration remains consequential. A nonresident owner may need U.S. tax returns for rental income, and FIRPTA can impose withholding obligations when a foreign person disposes of U.S. real property. Estate exposure, entity choice, financing, beneficial ownership, insurance and state rules can interact. Use independent U.S. and Florida legal and tax advisers rather than accepting a sales-team structure. The practical benefit of an international market is access to capable advisers—not permission to skip them.

Underwrite the building, the water and the policy

Florida title can be familiar and transparent, but buyer eligibility still needs the Chapter 692 review described above. For a condominium, title is only one layer. Obtain the declaration, bylaws, current budget, reserve schedule, financial statements, insurance, claims history, board minutes, engineering reports, violations, litigation, arrears, rental rules and pending assessments. Confirm whether parking, storage and amenities are deeded, assigned or revocable. Read the master policy beside an HO-6 quotation so gaps in wind, water, loss assessment and interior coverage are visible before the inspection period expires.

Florida's milestone-inspection and structural-integrity-reserve regime makes building age and governance financially material. Residential condominium and cooperative buildings of three or more habitable stories generally enter milestone inspection at 30 years, or 25 years when the local enforcement agency determines local conditions require it, and then at least every ten years. Structural integrity reserve studies also recur at least every ten years and cover defined structural and building systems. A completed report does not mean the cost disappears: ask what work was identified, funded, contracted, completed and still disputed.

Flood and storm exposure must be resolved at the address. Miami-Dade provides current FEMA flood-zone lookup and warns that being outside a mapped high-risk zone does not mean no flood risk. Obtain the elevation certificate where relevant, identify floor and critical-equipment elevation, inspect drainage and seawall responsibility, and quote flood, wind and homeowners coverage for the exact occupancy. Citizens' current phase-in generally requires flood coverage in 2026 for personal residential wind policies on dwellings with replacement value of at least $400,000, with broader requirements scheduled for 2027; condominium unit-owner policies are listed as exempt from that requirement. This is not a substitute for lender or private-insurer terms.

House diligence is different but no lighter. Inspect roof age and permit history, impact openings, electrical and plumbing systems, drainage, trees, pool equipment, sewer or septic, boundary and unpermitted work. For waterfront property, determine seawall condition, dock rights, permitting and maintenance responsibility. For every home, use current flood and evacuation lookups and ask the insurer what happens after a material claim. Insurance availability and deductible structure can alter financing, carrying cost and the future buyer pool even when the property itself appears sound.

Treat rental use as municipal permission, not a view

South Florida has deep long-term housing demand, but no destination-wide net yield should be applied to an individual property. Brickell rent is shaped by tower supply, association fees, parking and unit condition. Coral Gables and Coconut Grove homes reach different family and professional tenants. Fort Lauderdale beachfront condos may attract seasonal interest but also carry high association and insurance costs. Begin with a twelve-month tenancy supported by matched leases, then deduct vacancy, management, repairs, association charges, insurance, property tax, leasing fees and foreign-owner tax administration.

Vacation rental rules fragment at municipal and building level. Unincorporated Miami-Dade requires the relevant state licence and tax accounts plus an annual Certificate of Use before advertising. Miami Beach prohibits rentals shorter than six months and one day in all single-family homes and many multifamily districts; permitted cases still require local business and tax compliance. Fort Lauderdale requires state and county credentials before city registration, inspection and certificate steps, and limits occupancy by legal bedroom. An HOA or condominium declaration can be stricter than the city. Obtain written confirmation for the exact unit and intended stay length.

Capital upside should be separated from rental enthusiasm. The July 2026 public MLS signals show a broad and liquid region, but not a uniformly rising one: Miami-Dade existing condos had a $400,000 median and 12 months of supply, while Broward existing condos had a $255,000 median and 10 months. Miami-Dade single-family homes had a $685,000 median. These county-wide completed-sale signals exclude much new development and cannot value a particular address. They do show why building, submarket and property type must be analysed separately.

Underwrite three operating cases: lawful long-term tenancy, the exact permitted shorter-stay model, and no rent. In each, include owner-use blocks and realistic insurance and association increases. If only the optimistic short-stay case supports the purchase, the asset is not retirement-resilient. Capital strength is more credible when the home serves a broad resident buyer pool, the building has credible reserves and the future owner can insure it without extraordinary friction.

Price the complete ownership cycle and the next buyer

The three current asking observations show three distinct markets rather than a single Miami price. A Brickell one-bedroom asks $659,999 for 810 square feet of living area and carries a stated $1,319 monthly association charge. A Galt Ocean two-bedroom asks $849,000 for 1,650 square feet and carries a stated $1,594 monthly association charge. A renovated Coral Gables house asks $1.48 million for 1,575 square feet. On consistent seller-stated living area, the observations span roughly $5,500 to $10,100 per square metre. That spread is useful precisely because it exposes different operating systems; it is not a market median or valuation.

Model acquisition through exit. A two-percent acquisition-cost allowance can be a planning placeholder for buyer-side title, escrow, legal, inspection and financing work, but it is not a statutory quote and excludes property-specific surprises. Recalculate property tax after ownership changes because prior exemptions and assessed-value limits may not transfer; Florida guidance says a change of ownership removes exemptions and generally resets assessment to just value. Add insurance, flood cover, deductibles, association charges, reserves, assessments, repairs, utilities, health costs, management, travel, federal and state advice and sale costs.

Exit liquidity is strongest when the next buyer can understand and finance the home quickly. Brickell reaches an international and domestic apartment pool, yet elevated association charges or weak reserves can shrink it. Galt Ocean offers more area and direct coast but depends on the exact building record and an older-condo buyer's tolerance for costs. Coral Gables houses reach a broader owner-occupier pool but remain price-, insurance- and condition-sensitive. Ask two agents who did not source the property for matched completed sales, likely days to contract and the objections future buyers will raise.

For a foreign seller, FIRPTA withholding affects closing mechanics even when final tax differs. Estate and ownership structure may also change the result. Preserve original due-diligence records, permits, association disclosures, insurance history and improvement invoices so the next buyer can verify the asset. The best value entry is not the lowest unit price; it is the home whose daily utility, lawful use, five-year carrying cost and future buyer pool stay aligned under a slower market and a harder insurance renewal.

Buyer checklist—in decision order

  1. Confirm immigration, Medicare or private health, federal and Florida tax, estate and ownership structure before choosing a home.
  2. Obtain Florida legal clearance for the exact buyer, entity, parcel and Chapter 692 foreign-principal position before a non-refundable deposit.
  3. For a condominium, review inspections, reserve study, engineering, minutes, budget, insurance, claims, litigation, assessments and rental rules.
  4. For a house, inspect roof, impact openings, permits, drainage, electrical, plumbing, trees, pool, boundaries and any seawall or dock.
  5. Overlay current flood and evacuation maps, obtain elevation evidence where relevant and bind address-specific wind, flood, homeowners and liability terms.
  6. Obtain written municipality and association confirmation for the intended stay length, licence, certificate, tax, occupancy and manager duties.
  7. Recalculate property tax, model five-year owner costs and test lawful long-term rent, permitted short stay and no rent.
  8. Compare matched completed sales and ask two independent agents to identify the future buyer pool and likely objections under a slower market.
