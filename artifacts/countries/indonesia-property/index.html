Buying Property in Indonesia as a Foreigner

A practical acquisition guide for foreign buyers in Indonesia, from buyer eligibility and title checks to costs, ownership obligations and destination choice.

By Global Home Atlas Research Team · Published 2026-08-28 · Reviewed 2026-08-28

Can foreigners buy?

Foreign-buyer access in Indonesia depends on the buyer, title and property. Indonesia's Basic Agrarian Law reserves Hak Milik ownership to Indonesian citizens. Foreign residents may be eligible for Hak Pakai, while many marketed Bali villas are contractual leasehold interests. The exact land certificate, building rights, parties and registration must be independently verified.

Does ownership create residency?

Buying or leasing a Bali home does not itself create immigration status. Indonesia lists separate E33E and E33F retiree special-residency visas; eligibility, duration, sponsor or guarantee and financial conditions must be confirmed for the applicant before purchase.

Is financing practical?

Financing in Indonesia is lender- and borrower-specific. Obtain written terms covering eligibility, deposit, income, currency, valuation and property type before making a binding offer.

What limits short-term rentals?

OSS classifies villa accommodation under KBLI 55193. A residential building, online listing, local operator or seller forecast does not prove that the exact land use, building approvals, business entity, licence, tax and neighbourhood conditions support paid short stays.

Foreign freehold is not the default

A lease is a wasting contractual asset

A lease price must be read together with commencement, expiry, extension language, rent-reset method, landlord identity, succession, default, assignment and building ownership. An advertised extension option is not the same as a prepaid or enforceable extension at a known price.

How the purchase works

  1. 1

    Confirm the buyer, title and intended use

    Establish who can acquire the proposed title in Indonesia, who will be registered, and whether the property is for personal use, long-term rent or short stays.

  2. 2

    Appoint independent advisers

    Assign legal, tax, title, inspection, translation, finance and settlement responsibilities before paying a non-refundable deposit.

  3. 3

    Investigate the exact property

    Verify ownership, boundaries, planning, lawful construction, occupancy, leases, building condition, access, utilities, governance and address-level hazards.

  4. 4

    Review the contract before signing

    Confirm price, deposit, conditions, financing, defects, fixtures, possession, tax adjustments, cancellation rights and the documents required to transfer the chosen title.

  5. 5

    Settle and register the transfer

    Coordinate verified funds, final inspection, closing documents, taxes and registration; do not treat payment alone as proof that title transferred correctly.

  6. 6

    Operate the property after closing

    Put tax, insurance, utilities, management, repairs, building notices, rental compliance and future owner-record updates onto a written calendar.

Rules after purchase

Keep title and owner records current

Confirm the registered owner, title form, address and local registration requirements in Indonesia; assign ongoing owner administration before closing.

Budget for the building, management and repairs

Review building condition, condominium or community governance, management charges, reserves, insurance and planned repair work before committing.

Verify rental and lodging authority

OSS classifies villa accommodation under KBLI 55193. A residential building, online listing, local operator or seller forecast does not prove that the exact land use, building approvals, business entity, licence, tax and neighbourhood conditions support paid short stays.

Maintain the tax, hazard and insurance file

Confirm acquisition and annual tax obligations, address-level hazards and insurability; retain the records needed for ownership, rental activity and eventual sale.

Before making an offer

  • Confirm that this buyer can acquire this title in Indonesia.
  • Match the contract buyer to identity, address, bank and registration records.
  • Confirm intended use against planning, building and rental rules.
  • Resolve title, boundaries, security interests, occupancy and access.
  • Inspect the structure, services and address-level hazards.
  • Obtain a written total-cash requirement and financing decision.
  • Assign tax, insurance, management, repair and resale responsibilities.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner buy property in Indonesia?

It depends on the buyer, title and property. Start with the eligibility rules above, then have the exact asset and transaction verified before signing.

Does buying property in Indonesia create residency?

Buying or leasing a Bali home does not itself create immigration status. Indonesia lists separate E33E and E33F retiree special-residency visas; eligibility, duration, sponsor or guarantee and financial conditions must be confirmed for the applicant before purchase.

What should be checked before making an offer?

Confirm buyer eligibility, title, lawful construction and use, building condition, hazards, total costs, financing, tax, rental authority and the likely resale pool.
