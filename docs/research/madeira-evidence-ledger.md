## Editorial limits

- Asking observations are examples, not recommendations or valuations.
- Completed-sale medians describe municipalities and cannot price an individual address.
- Regional risk evidence does not replace current municipal layers, an engineer, or an insurance quotation.
- Healthcare references establish systems and units, not personal eligibility or treatment availability.
- The scheduled dossier review is 2027-02-22, with earlier review whenever a law, municipal rule, listing, transport service, hazard source, or statistics release changes.
