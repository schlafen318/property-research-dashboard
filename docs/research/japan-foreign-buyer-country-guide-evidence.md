Reviewed: 2026-08-27

This ledger records the official material used for the acquisition guide. It does not replace transaction-specific legal, tax, immigration, lending, engineering, or insurance advice.

## Editorial conclusions

- A foreign individual living overseas can be recorded as an owner when the specified identity, Roman-letter name, address-evidence, translation, and registration requirements are met. This supports “generally buy and register,” not an absolute promise for every property or regulated land category.
- Fukuoka City's official FAQ expressly separates real-estate ownership from Japanese residence or immigration status. Together with the ISA definition of residence status and MOFA's route-specific eligibility, this supports the guide's decisive answer that purchase does not create residence rights and that buyers must qualify through an immigration route.
- The official sources do not publish a universal mortgage eligibility rule for non-resident buyers. The guide labels financing lender-specific and requires written lender terms before a binding offer; it does not claim a guaranteed product or rate.
- No universal closing-cost percentage is used. Acquisition, registration, brokerage/professional, ownership, non-resident tax-administration, and eventual sale costs are separated because they arise from different facts and dates.
