## Editorial decision rules

- Market anchors remain public registered-sale signals, never valuations.
- The Begur figure is always labelled new homes and the 26-sale sample is disclosed.
- Listing evidence remains EUR-first, direct, dated and non-recommended.
- Tourist-rental income is excluded unless the exact municipal, community and operating route is cleared.
- Healthcare claims distinguish Girona regional care from address-specific coastal access.
- Transport claims distinguish Girona's rail spine from the car- or bus-led coast.
- A home outside a mapped hazard area is never described as risk-free.
