# Los Angeles / Orange County evidence ledger

Reviewed 2026-08-27. Recheck by 2027-02-27 and after material federal, California, city, insurance, hazard or listing changes.

| Decision area | Evidence used | Reader consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership and exit | IRS FIRPTA guidance; California FTB real-estate and nonresident withholding | Title is generally accessible, but ownership does not create residence and foreign sellers need an exit-withholding plan. |
| Property tax | California Board of Equalization supplemental-assessment guidance; Los Angeles and Orange County assessor records | A purchase can reset assessed value and create supplemental bills; the seller's current tax is not the buyer's forecast. |
| Short stays | Los Angeles City Planning Home-Sharing program | Los Angeles home sharing is limited to an eligible registered primary residence; other cities and associations require separate checks. |
| Insurance and hazards | California Department of Insurance residential guide and FAIR Plan material | Secure property-specific homeowners, earthquake and wildfire indications before removing contingencies. |
| Access | LA Metro LAX guidance; John Wayne Airport nonstop destinations | Airport depth is strong, but last-mile time and mode depend on the exact submarket. |
| Market direction | FHFA 2026 Q2 HPI; Orange County 2025–26 assessment roll | These show broad direction and taxable-value scale, not the value of an individual home. |
| Property observations | Three direct Redfin listing pages | The sample spans Long Beach, Santa Monica and Newport Coast; all are dated asking evidence only. |
