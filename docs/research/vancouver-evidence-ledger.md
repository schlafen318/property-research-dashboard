# Vancouver evidence ledger

Reviewed 2026-08-27. Recheck by 2027-02-27 and after material federal, provincial, municipal, market, hazard or listing changes.

| Decision area | Evidence used | Reader consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase eligibility | Department of Finance Canada extension; CMHC prohibition FAQ | Most non-Canadians cannot buy covered Vancouver homes before 2027; exceptions need a written legal opinion before an offer. |
| Transfer and vacancy taxes | B.C. additional property transfer tax; B.C. Speculation and Vacancy Tax; City of Vancouver Empty Homes Tax | An eligible foreign buyer can face a 20% transfer surcharge and separate annual vacancy-tax regimes. |
| Federal annual tax | CRA Underused Housing Tax notice | UHT filing and payment were eliminated for 2025 onward, but earlier obligations can remain relevant to an existing foreign owner. |
| Short stays | B.C. principal-residence requirement and consolidated short-term-rental law | Vancouver short stays are not an absentee-income strategy; provincial registration, principal residence and local or strata rules apply. |
| Healthcare | Vancouver Coastal Health VGH services and non-resident payments | The city has tertiary care, but ownership does not create MSP eligibility or free care. |
| Access | YVR public-transport guidance | The Canada Line reaches downtown in under 30 minutes; the exact last mile still changes the recommendation. |
| Hazards and buildings | City of Vancouver earthquake scenario and designated floodplain data | Seismic and flood evidence must be overlaid with building-specific envelope, drainage, insurance and reserve diligence. |
| Market anchors | Greater Vancouver REALTORS May 2026 report | The three benchmarks provide geography and property-type context only, not valuations of the observations. |
| Property observations | Three direct Redfin pages | The sample spans Yaletown, Lower Lonsdale and Point Grey; all are dated asks, not completed sales. |
