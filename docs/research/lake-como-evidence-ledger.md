## Editorial limits

- Asking observations are examples, not recommendations or valuations.
- OMI figures are official zone ranges; they do not price a specific apartment, villa or view.
- National and regional rules do not replace the current file of the exact comune and condominium.
- Official hazard mapping does not replace a survey, drainage and slope review, claims history or an insurance quotation.
- Healthcare references establish systems and facilities, not personal entitlement or availability of a particular treatment.
- The scheduled dossier review is 2027-02-22, with earlier review whenever a law, municipal rule, listing, transport service, hazard source or statistics release changes.
