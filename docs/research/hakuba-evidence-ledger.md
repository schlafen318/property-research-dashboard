## Editorial decision rules

- Market anchors remain labelled as public signals, never valuations.
- Listing evidence remains JPY-first, dated, direct and non-recommended.
- A rental claim is excluded unless the exact legal route, building, operator and costs can be checked.
- A healthcare claim distinguishes village clinics from regional emergency and specialist care.
- A transport claim distinguishes permanent public transport from seasonal ski shuttles.
- A property outside a mapped hazard area is never described as risk-free.
