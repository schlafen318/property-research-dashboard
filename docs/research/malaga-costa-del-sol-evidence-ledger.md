## Editorial limits

- Asking observations are examples, not recommendations or valuations.
- Registered provincial averages do not price a Málaga apartment, Fuengirola flat or Estepona house.
- Regional and national risk evidence does not replace current municipal layers, an engineer or an insurance quotation.
- Healthcare references establish systems and facilities, not personal eligibility or treatment availability.
- The scheduled dossier review is 2027-02-22, with earlier review whenever a law, municipal rule, listing, transport service, hazard source or statistics release changes.
