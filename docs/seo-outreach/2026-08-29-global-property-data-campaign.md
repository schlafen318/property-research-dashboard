# Global property data outreach brief

## Asset

- Page: `https://globalhomeatlas.com/global-property-market-data/`
- CSV: `https://globalhomeatlas.com/data/global-property-market-data.csv`
- Angle: a downloadable, consistently scored comparison of 37 international property markets covering price, estimated net yield, buyer access, ownership clarity, regulatory safety, retirement fit, foreigner fit and exit liquidity.

## Best-fit publishers

Prioritize pages where the dataset adds evidence rather than merely supplying a link:

1. International property and housing-market publications updating global comparisons.
2. Expat, relocation and retirement publications maintaining destination resources.
3. Data journalists covering affordability, cross-border ownership or second homes.
4. Independent buyer advisers publishing research libraries or client resources.
5. University, library and research-resource pages cataloguing international housing data.

## Qualification rules

- The site has an identifiable editor or author and a relevant existing article or resource page.
- The proposed placement helps the reader verify or extend a claim already on that page.
- The publisher is editorially independent; exclude paid-link networks, bulk directories and sites selling followed links.
- The page is indexable, maintained and topically aligned with cross-border property research.
- Personalize the note with one specific table, claim or outdated passage the dataset can improve.

## Pitch template

Subject: Downloadable data for your [topic] resource

Hi [name],

I noticed your [article/resource] compares [specific markets or buyer question]. Global Home Atlas has published a downloadable comparison of 37 international property markets using one framework for price, estimated net yield, ownership clarity, buyer access, retirement fit and exit liquidity:

https://globalhomeatlas.com/global-property-market-data/

The underlying CSV is available on the page, and the methodology and limitations are public. The [specific column/market comparison] may be useful when you next update [specific passage]. If you use it, attribution to Global Home Atlas and the dataset page helps readers inspect the source.

Best,
[name]

## Tracking fields

Maintain one row per qualified opportunity:

| Field | Use |
|---|---|
| Publisher | Domain or publication |
| Contact | Editor or author |
| Source page | Relevant existing URL |
| Fit note | Exact claim or section the data improves |
| Contact date | First message date |
| Follow-up date | One follow-up, if appropriate |
| Status | Researched, pitched, replied, placed, declined |
| Result URL | Published citation or link |
| Link type | Editorial, resource page, mention without link |
| Notes | Response and next action |

Do not automate bulk outreach. Send only individually qualified notes and stop after one relevant follow-up.
