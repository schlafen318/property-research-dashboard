# Crete Premium Dossier Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Replace Crete's generic destination page with a publish-ready premium dossier that earns every hard gate in the destination rulebook before deployment.

## Content contract

- Preserve the canonical destination data and ten consolidated Atlas scores.
- Use five paired lenses and 1,800–2,500 words of locally specific decision prose.
- Distinguish Chania and the Akrotiri coast, Apokoronas, Rethymno, Heraklion, and eastern Crete around Agios Nikolaos / Elounda.
- Cover residence separately from ownership; AFM, transfer tax, cadastral/title and planning diligence; short-term-rental registration; health access; airports and island travel; wildfire, heat, water, earthquake and flood exposure; seasonality; maintenance; value and exit.
- Provide three bounded July 2026 asking-price anchors, three current direct residential listing observations, four micro-location patterns, two orientation sequences, eight ordered checklist items, and references last.
- Use primary government, tax, migration, cadastral, health, civil-protection and central-bank sources for high-stakes claims. Treat portal data and listings as asking evidence only.
